# Notifications

> In-app alerts for social interactions, price alerts, and reminders

## Overview

Open **Notifications** from the top bar or sidebar badge to review in-app notifications. The badge shows unread count and refreshes while signed in.

## Notification Types

- **Social activity** — Follow requests, accepted follows, comments, and ratings.
- **Wishlist availability** — Scheduled/manual availability checks can notify when wishlist items become unavailable.
- **Auction alerts** — Price alerts, bid reminders, auction ending reminders, and watch-bid digests. A price alert states the current high bid against the target it crossed, and links its lot number to the auction site. The watch-bid digest reports each lot's current high bid against the bid the previous digest reported, so it says what moved rather than just what it is, and links each lot number to the lot on the auction site.
- **New auction lots** — One batched alert when the background watchlist sync starts tracking lots you watched or bid on at NumisBids or CNG; opens the lot directly from the notification.
- **Now bidding** — One batched alert when the sync sees a bid of yours appear on a lot you were only watching, with the current high bid and your max bid.
- **Outbid** — One batched alert when the provider reports someone else holding the winning bid on a lot you are bidding on, with where the bidding stands and how long is left. Fires once per time you lose the lead, and again if you retake and lose it once more.
- **Coin of the Day** — Daily featured coin notification for opted-in users.
- **Set milestones** — Coin set milestone notifications when configured thresholds are crossed.

## Actions

- Mark notifications as read
- Delete notifications
- Open linked detail views where a notification includes a reference
- Open Coin of the Day directly in the featured coin modal
